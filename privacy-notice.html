Privacy notice

Last updated 29 October 2026

This notice explains how Logo Hero collects and uses your personal information when you visit logohero.co.uk, get in touch, or place an order.

Who we are

Logo Hero is the data controller for the personal information described here.

For anything about your data, email branding@logohero.co.uk.

What we collect and why

We only collect what we need to reply to you, deliver your order and run the site. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data.

PurposeWhat we collectLegal basis (UK GDPR)
Replying to enquiries Your name, email address, anything you tell us, and any logo files you send Legitimate interests (answering you) or steps you ask for before ordering
Taking and delivering your order Name, email address, business name, website address, and the logo files you supply Contract
Taking payment Payment details entered directly with Stripe. We never see your full card number. We receive confirmation of payment, the amount, and your billing name and email Contract
Keeping financial records Order and payment records Legal obligation
Running and securing the website Your IP address and basic technical data (browser, device, pages requested) in hosting logs Legitimate interests (keeping the site working and secure)

You don't have to give us any of this, but we can't deliver an order without the order details.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the services that help us run Logo Hero:

  • Stripe processes payments. Stripe is responsible for the payment details you give it; see Stripe's privacy policy. Stripe may set cookies on the payment page to prevent fraud.
  • Formspree receives the details you enter on our order form and passes them to us; see Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the service that starts your payment with Stripe, so your name, email and business name pass through it; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft handles our email, including anything you send us.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and keeps standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on our pages, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads and Google sees your IP address; see Google's privacy policy.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where your data is transferred outside the UK, it is protected by the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual safeguards approved under UK law.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example to HMRC or a court.

Your rights and complaints

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your information, where we have no good reason to keep it
  • restrict or stop how we use it, including objecting to uses based on legitimate interests
  • send you your information in a portable format, where it was given under a contract

Email branding@logohero.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Cookies and changes to this notice

Our own pages don't set cookies. The only cookies you may meet are strictly necessary ones set by Stripe on the payment page, which don't need your consent.

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
